          Snap a purchase

          Upload a screenshot of a purchase — a card app's transaction detail, a payment confirmation, a statement row. Everything read is shown for you to correct before anything is saved. The image never leaves this machine.

          What will this cost?

          The latest published euro rate, fetched now, with your card's foreign transaction fee on top. It is a daily reference rate, not a live tick — and a card does not settle at the rate you see anyway: Visa converts on the day it processes the purchase, and the fee follows.

          —

          Cards and accounts

          The fee is a property of the card, not of the purchase — the same euro coffee costs 2.5% more on a CIBC Visa than on a euro account. The published figure is a starting point; once statements are imported the real markup is measured from your own rows.
